# Personal homepage

The personal homepage is published at <https://dongdaoyang.github.io/>. Residual Stress Hub is a separate site at <https://rshub-am.github.io/>, maintained in the `rshub-am/rshub-am.github.io` repository.

## Editing

- `_pages/about.md`: biography, research interests, featured projects, education, and honors.
- `_pages/publications.md` and `_data/personal_publications.json`: publications, ordered by year. Set `selected: true` to feature a paper on the homepage.
- `_pages/projects.md`: research software and resources.
- `assets/css/personal-home.css`: site-specific presentation; no theme layouts or includes are overridden.
- `_pages/hub-redirects/`: compatibility redirects for the former Hub URLs. Keep these when editing the personal site.

The local personal-site directory is `dongdaoyang.github.io`; the independent Hub directory is `rshub-am.github.io`. Check `git remote -v` before publishing.

## Publication scope

Only works from 2025 onward arising from the HKUST period are displayed. Publications from the Southeast University period are excluded, even when their publication year is 2025 or later. Education history is retained.
