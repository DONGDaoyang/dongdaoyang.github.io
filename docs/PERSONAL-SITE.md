# Personal homepage

The personal homepage is published at <https://dongdaoyang.github.io/>.

## Editing

- `_pages/about.md`: biography, research interests, featured projects, education, and honors.
- `_pages/publications.md` and `_data/personal_publications.json`: publications, ordered by year. Set `selected: true` to feature a paper on the homepage.
- `_pages/projects.md`: research software and resources.
- `assets/css/personal-home.css`: site-specific presentation; no theme layouts or includes are overridden.

The local personal-site directory is `dongdaoyang.github.io`. Check `git remote -v` before publishing.

## Publication scope

Only published journal articles from 2025 onward arising from the HKUST period are displayed. Preprints are excluded. Publications from the Southeast University period are excluded, even when their publication year is 2025 or later. Education history is retained.

## Project scope

Show only software personally developed or maintained by Daoyang Dong. Coauthorship of a paper does not imply authorship of its code. Do not list Residual Stress Hub as a personal project or add links or redirects to it from this personal website.
