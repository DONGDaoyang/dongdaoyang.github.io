Double-cantilever (LPBF · IN718)

This is the IN718 double-cantilever model. A local thermal–mechanical simulation is used to identify inherent strain, and the model calculates residual stress and warpage after releasing the left and right interfaces.

Overview

This is the IN718 double-cantilever model. A local thermal–mechanical simulation is used to identify inherent strain, and the model calculates residual stress and warpage after releasing the left and right interfaces. [1]
