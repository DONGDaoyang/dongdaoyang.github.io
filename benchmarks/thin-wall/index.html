Overview

This is a four-layer S355 steel thin-wall model. The thermal–mechanical analysis follows successive line scans and cooling stages, then releases the specified anchors to calculate springback. The original thin-wall benchmark and experimental results are reported by Ding et al. [1]

Results

Springback after release

The maximum release-induced displacement increment is 3.246338 mm, with a vertical component of 3.242710 mm at that node. It measures the change between the states before and after release, rather than the total released displacement shown in the profile.
