Cantilever (LPBF · IN625)

A printed cantilever: local thermal–mechanical response, inherent-strain calibration, residual elastic strain, and distortion after release.

Residual Stress Hub

← All benchmarks

Overview

This is the AM-Bench 2018-1 IN625 cantilever model. After printing, residual stresses were measured using neutron diffraction and the contour method. Warpage displacement was measured after separating the cantilever from its supports. [1]
