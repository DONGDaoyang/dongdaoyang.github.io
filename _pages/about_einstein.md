I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from Southeast University in 2021 and 2024, respectively. In February 2025, I joined the Computational Mechanics and Scientific Learning (CMSL) Lab at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

My research interests lie in metal additive manufacturing, with a focus on process simulation, manufacturing process-related problems, and computational acceleration techniques for efficient numerical modeling.
