ABOUT THIS HUB

A subject-first space for research.

This Hub organizes residual stress computational cases, not a personal project portfolio. Each case brings together its problem definition, model setup, results, and related literature. Its initial scope is metal additive manufacturing and numerical modeling. It is independently maintained; it is not an official university, laboratory, or standards-body platform.

External references are attributed to their original sources. A computational case is not described as a validated benchmark unless its specification and evidence support that claim.
